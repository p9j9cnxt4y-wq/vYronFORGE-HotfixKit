# Manufacturer policy review — 2026-11-02

The current snapshot is a selected advisory review, not a live or exhaustive vulnerability feed. `PASS_FIXED` concerns the named policy only; it does not attest a host, supported lifecycle or absence of later vulnerabilities.

The September snapshot remains byte-identical. CERT-EU mappings and related-CVE fields are historical references, not newly verified coverage. Exploitation status UNKNOWN does not mean no exploitation. Historical risk priority is retained conservatively; it is not promoted to a fresh exploitation claim.
